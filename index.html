💡 Honest Industry Truth

Clear, honest transparency about funerals.

In Australia, families are often confused about who does what when someone dies. We keep it completely transparent:

1. What We Do (The Ceremony)

We craft, curate, rehearse, and lead the human ceremony. We meet with family, weave stories, coordinate music and audio, and guide the room. You pay us our flat fee with no retail product markups.

2. What The Funeral Director Does

A licensed mortuary director looks after the physical care of the deceased, transport, death certificates, and the cemetery/cremation bookings. They quote and invoice you directly for those exact logistics.

This means you get an unhurried, genuinely personal service without feeling pressured into buying velvet-lined coffins or packages you never asked for.
